# Cargo Claims

Doc ID: KFL-CLM-01 · Revision: 2025-07 · Status: Current

## Filing window

A cargo claim must be filed within 9 months of the delivery date.

## Concealed damage

Damage that was not visible at delivery must be reported within 5 business days of delivery. Reports made later are reviewed but carry a presumption that the damage happened after delivery.

## How to file

Claims are filed only through the Claims Portal, using form `CL-10`. Claims are not accepted by phone or by email.

## What to include

A claim needs the signed bill of lading, the delivery receipt with exceptions noted, photos of the damage, and the commercial invoice showing the value of the goods.

## Liability limit

Unless a higher value was declared at booking, liability is limited to $25 per pound for new goods and $0.50 per pound for used goods.

## Timeline

Kestrel acknowledges a claim within 30 days and resolves it within 120 days.
