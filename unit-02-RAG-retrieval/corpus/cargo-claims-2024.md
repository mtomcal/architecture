# Cargo Claims (2024)

Doc ID: KFL-CLM-01 · Revision: 2024-03 · Status: Superseded by revision 2025-07

## Filing window

A cargo claim must be filed within 9 months of the delivery date.

## Concealed damage

Damage that was not visible at delivery must be reported within 15 days of delivery.

## How to file

Claims may be filed by email to the claims desk or by phone with a claims representative.

## Timeline

Kestrel acknowledges a claim within 30 days and resolves it within 120 days.
