# On-Time Guarantee

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-06 · Revision: 2025-01 · Status: Current

## Which shipments are covered

The on-time guarantee applies only to Kestrel Priority shipments. Standard and Dedicated shipments carry no late-delivery credit.

## Credits

If a Priority shipment delivers more than 2 hours after the committed time, the customer receives a credit of 25% of the linehaul charge. If it delivers on a later calendar day, the credit is 100% of the linehaul charge.

## Exclusions

No credit is owed when the delay was caused by weather, a road closure, a receiver that could not unload on time, or detention at an earlier stop on the same load.

## How to claim a credit

The customer requests the credit through the customer service desk within 15 days of delivery. Credits are applied to the invoice and are never paid in cash.
