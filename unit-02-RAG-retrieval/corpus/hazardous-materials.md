# Hazardous Materials

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-09 · Revision: 2025-03 · Status: Current

## Classes accepted

Kestrel accepts hazard classes 3 (flammable liquids), 8 (corrosives) and 9 (miscellaneous). Kestrel does not carry class 1 (explosives) or class 7 (radioactive) under any service.

## Driver requirements

The driver must hold a current hazmat endorsement. Shipping papers stay within the driver's reach while driving and on the driver's seat when the driver leaves the cab.

## Shipping papers

Papers must show the proper shipping name, hazard class, UN number, packing group and a 24-hour emergency phone number.

## Surcharge

Hazmat shipments carry a handling surcharge of $125 per shipment. Billing code: `ACC-HM`.

## Service restrictions

Hazmat is not accepted on Kestrel Priority. It moves on Standard or Dedicated service only.
