# Accessorial Charges

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-04 · Revision: 2025-06 · Status: Current

## Schedule of charges

| Service | Code | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Liftgate | `ACC-LG` | $85 per stop |
| Inside delivery | `ACC-IN` | $120 per stop |
| Residential delivery | `ACC-RES` | $95 per stop |
| Limited-access location | `ACC-LA` | $110 per stop |
| Driver assist (loading or unloading) | `ACC-DA` | $60 per hour, 1-hour minimum |
| Redelivery | `ACC-RD` | See Redelivery, KFL-OPS-05 |
| Hazmat handling | `ACC-HM` | See Hazardous Materials, KFL-OPS-09 |

## Limited-access locations

Limited-access locations include schools, military bases, construction sites, farms and self-storage facilities.

## When charges must be requested

Accessorials requested at booking are billed at the scheduled rate. Accessorials added by the driver at the stop need a phone approval from the customer service desk before the service is performed.
