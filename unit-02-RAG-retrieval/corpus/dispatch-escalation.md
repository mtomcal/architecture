# Dispatch and Escalation

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-14 · Revision: 2025-06 · Status: Current

## Who to contact

Day dispatch runs 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. local time. After-hours dispatch covers nights, weekends and holidays.

## Escalation ladder

1. Dispatcher on duty.
2. Dispatch supervisor, if the issue is unresolved after 30 minutes.
3. Terminal manager, for anything involving an accident, a hazmat spill or a rejected load.

## Rejected loads

When a receiver rejects a load, the driver does not leave until dispatch gives disposition instructions. The driver gets the reason for rejection in writing on the bill of lading.

## Accidents and spills

The driver calls emergency services first, then after-hours dispatch. Dispatch notifies the terminal manager immediately.
