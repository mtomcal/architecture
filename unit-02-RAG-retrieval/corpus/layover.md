# Layover

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-02 · Revision: 2025-02 · Status: Current

## When layover applies

Layover applies when a driver is held past the 8-hour detention cap, or when a load is rescheduled to the next calendar day by the shipper or receiver. Billing code: `LAY-1`.

## Rate

Layover bills at $350 per 24-hour period, or any part of one.

## Detention and layover together

Detention and layover are not billed for the same hours. When layover starts, detention stops.

## Approval

Layover must be approved by the customer service desk before it is billed. Dispatch records the approval number on the load.
