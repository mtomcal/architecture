# Fuel Surcharge

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-12 · Revision: 2025-05 · Status: Current

## How it is set

The fuel surcharge is a percentage of the linehaul charge. It is reset every Monday from the national average diesel price published that week by the U.S. Department of Energy.

## Bands

| Diesel price per gallon | Surcharge |
|---|---|
| Under $3.00 | 0% |
| $3.00 to $3.49 | 12% |
| $3.50 to $3.99 | 16% |
| $4.00 to $4.49 | 20% |
| $4.50 and over | 24% |

## Where to find the current rate

The surcharge in effect this week is posted on the customer portal each Monday. It is not listed in this handbook.

## What it applies to

The surcharge applies to linehaul only. It does not apply to accessorial charges, detention or layover.
