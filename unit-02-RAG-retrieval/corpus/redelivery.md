# Redelivery

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-05 · Revision: 2025-06 · Status: Current

## When redelivery applies

A redelivery is a second delivery attempt after the first one failed because the receiver was closed, refused the freight, or had no appointment on file.

## Charge

Redelivery bills at a flat $150 plus $2.10 per mile from the nearest Kestrel terminal to the receiver. Billing code: `ACC-RD`.

## Authorization

Every redelivery needs an authorization number from the customer service desk before the freight leaves the terminal. Authorization numbers start with `RD-`.

## Storage before redelivery

Freight waiting for redelivery is held at the terminal free for 2 business days. After that, storage bills at $40 per pallet per day.

## When redelivery is not charged

Redelivery is not charged when the first attempt failed because of a Kestrel error, such as arriving outside the appointment window.
