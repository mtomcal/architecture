# Service Levels

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-13 · Revision: 2025-01 · Status: Current

## Standard

Scheduled transit with no delivery-time commitment. Pickup requests received by 2:00 p.m. local time are picked up the same day.

## Kestrel Priority

Guaranteed delivery by the committed time, backed by the on-time guarantee. Pickup requests must be received by 11:00 a.m. local time. Priority is not available for hazmat freight.

## Dedicated

A truck and driver assigned to one customer for a contracted period. Rates, free time and accessorials for Dedicated service are set by the customer's contract, and the contract overrides this handbook wherever they differ.
