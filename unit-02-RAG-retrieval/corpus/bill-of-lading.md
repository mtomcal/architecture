# Bill of Lading

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-11 · Revision: 2025-01 · Status: Current

## The form

Kestrel uses form `BOL-7` for all shipments unless the shipper supplies its own bill of lading.

## Required fields

Shipper and receiver names and addresses, piece count, weight, freight class, seal number, and any special instructions such as temperature setpoint or hazmat details.

## At pickup

The driver counts pieces, checks the seal number against the form, and signs. Any shortage or visible damage is written on the form before the driver leaves.

## At delivery

The receiver signs and writes any exceptions, such as shortage or damage. A bill of lading signed without exceptions is treated as a clean delivery.

## Proof of delivery

The driver uploads the signed bill of lading within 24 hours of delivery.
