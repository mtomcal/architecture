# Appointments and Check-In

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-10 · Revision: 2025-02 · Status: Current

## Appointment windows

Deliveries and pickups are made by appointment unless the facility is listed as first come, first served.

## Checking in

Drivers may check in up to 30 minutes before the appointment. A driver who checks in no later than 30 minutes after the appointment time is on time.

## Late arrival

A driver who checks in more than 30 minutes after the appointment time is late and is unloaded on a work-in basis. For a late driver, detention free time starts when the trailer is at the dock, not at the appointment time.

## First come, first served facilities

At these facilities, free time starts at check-in.

## Missed appointments

If the driver cannot make the appointment, dispatch must call the facility at least 2 hours ahead to reschedule. A missed appointment caused by Kestrel is not billable to the customer.
