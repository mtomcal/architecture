# Detention

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-01 · Revision: 2025-02 · Status: Current

## What detention is

Detention is the charge for holding a driver and trailer at a shipper or receiver beyond the allowed free time. Billing code: `DET-2`.

## Free time

Each stop includes 2 hours of free time. Free time starts at the scheduled appointment time, not at the driver's arrival time, provided the driver checked in on time.

## Rate

After free time ends, detention bills at $75 per hour, charged in 15-minute increments.

## Cap

Detention is capped at 8 billable hours per stop. A driver held beyond the cap is moved to layover (see Layover, KFL-OPS-02).

## Proof required

Detention is only billable when the bill of lading shows both an in time and an out time, each signed or stamped by the facility. Without both times, dispatch cannot bill detention and the charge is waived.
