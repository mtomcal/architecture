# Temperature-Controlled Loads

Doc ID: KFL-OPS-03 · Revision: 2025-04 · Status: Current

## Scope

This policy covers refrigerated and frozen freight moved in reefer trailers. Where it differs from a general policy, this policy wins.

## Temperature requirements

The setpoint is taken from the bill of lading. Allowed variance is plus or minus 2°F. The reefer unit must run in continuous mode, never cycle mode, unless the shipper states otherwise in writing.

## Records

Drivers download the reefer temperature log at delivery. Receivers may take pulp temperatures. A rejected load must have the pulp readings written on the bill of lading.

## Free time and detention for reefer loads

Because the unit burns fuel while waiting, reefer loads get 1 hour of free time per stop, not the standard allowance. Reefer detention bills at $95 per hour in 15-minute increments. The 8-hour cap and the proof requirements in the Detention policy still apply.

## Fuel for the reefer unit

Reefer fuel is included in the linehaul rate and is not billed separately.
